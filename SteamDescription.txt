Unofficial fixes and tweaks for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3220299022]Dynamic Diplomacy - Continued[/url]. The images above show how a conquest battle plays out, what happens to captives, how dominant factions are opposed, and how expansion and ruins work.

[h2]Battles[/h2]
[list]
[*]Conquest battles in long saves no longer throw [i]System.OverflowException ... at DynamicDiplomacy.IncidentWorker_NPCConquest.InitArenaMap[/i] (about 37.5% of them did, and auto-calc decided).
[*]Basic arena AI: the attacker hunts the nearest defenders, the defender holds near its entry, instead of both walking to the map center.
[*]Semi-simulation: a battle site nobody enters within 2 days is fought out on its map instead of decided by auto-calc. Caravans can enter a site before its map exists.
[*]Optional: battles on the real settlement map, defended by its own garrison ([url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3813689040]Custom Base Framework[/url] bases and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3771713589]Better Settlement Defense[/url] roles apply). Default 25% of battles.
[*]If one side is the dominant faction (more than 9 settlements and 3 times the average faction's count or 40% of all), an ally of the other side joins with half the threat points.
[*]Optional: the stronger the defender, the likelier pirates drop in on it. The gap 1 - attacker / defender combat power is the chance and the pirates' share of the threat points, up to half. On by default.
[*]Optional: survivors return to the world instead of being deleted, and downed losers become the winner's captives. Captives may join the captor (30 days), escape (60 days) or go free when the factions make peace or the captor is defeated; unwaveringly loyal ones never join. On by default.
[*]Once one army flees, the other holds the field. A win for a settlement lost meanwhile changes nothing.
[*]Battle sites: no longer owned by you, listed in "Jump to location", and their pollution costs you no goodwill.
[/list]

[h2]Diplomacy[/h2]
[list]
[*]The "great alliance" now works with simulated conquest, honours "exclude Empire" and permanent enemies, and uses the dominance rule above.
[*]Goodwill follows war declarations by rebellions and battles, so wars no longer end silently.
[*]Defeated rebel factions are removed once nothing uses them; the oldest of each kind stays.
[*]Optional: NPC factions make peace with or turn on you a set number of times per year. Off by default.
[/list]

[h2]Expansion and ruins[/h2]
[list]
[*]The expanding faction is picked by tech level: tribal 1.5, medieval 1.3, industrial 1, spacer 0.8, ultra 0.6. On by default.
[*]Half of the expansions resettle a ruin near an NPC settlement instead of a random tile.
[*]Clearing ruins keeps ruins on roads, so roads do not end in empty land.
[*]A button next to the expansion limit clears a world above it down to 80%: each faction in proportion, keeping at least 2, off-road settlements first, ruins kept on roads.
[*]Conquered and razed settlements are destroyed the normal way, so other mods clean up after them (World Domination's buildings).
[*]Event frequency settings go below the old 1% per 6 hours minimum, and the settings warn when an option has no effect.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]A fresh install starts from recommended Dynamic Diplomacy settings (simulated conquest, raze 50% with ruin clearing, among others). Saved settings are unchanged.
[*]Safe to add to an existing save. Remove it only when no arena battle is running and no caravan is heading to one.
[*]Load after Harmony and Dynamic Diplomacy - Continued.
[*]"Reached max signals per frame (3000)" during NPC siege camps is a warning instead of an error.
[*]Dev mode: Debug actions > "Dynamic Diplomacy Fix" starts arenas, skips waits and runs the cleanup or a player diplomacy change.
[/list]
